CrossDink Theme Generator

Builds a theme.json for an SD theme pack. Layout metrics only; nothing here touches refresh modes, waveforms or panel registers.

Touch devices (X4 Pro, Sticky) reject the classic and roundedraff bases.
480x800, 1-bit grays. Approximation of the firmware drawing: row heights, gaps, radii and styles follow the metrics, fonts and artwork do not.

theme.json

Only values that differ from the base are written. Put the file at /.themes/<name>/theme.json (or /themes/<name>/) on the SD card, then pick it under Settings > Display > Theme pack. Values outside the safe range are clamped here, as the firmware does. Icons are not generated: add 24 or 32 px 1-bit BMPs by hand and list them under icons.
